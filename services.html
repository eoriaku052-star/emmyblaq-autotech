Why your vehicle model and year matter

Vehicles from the same manufacturer can have different wiring layouts, control modules and AC components across model years and trim levels. Sharing the make, model and year helps us understand your enquiry and discuss the appropriate service.

How Our Service Process Works

We make the next step clear before repair work begins.

  1. Tell us what is happening. Explain the symptoms and share your vehicle make, model and year.
  2. Inspection and testing. We inspect the affected system and carry out relevant checks.
  3. Explanation and quotation. We explain our findings and provide a quotation based on the job.
  4. Your approval. Repair work begins after the proposed work and price have been discussed with you.
  5. Repair and checks. We carry out the agreed work and appropriate checks related to the service.

You can ask questions about the findings and proposed work before deciding how to proceed.
