Relay information

Relay positions can vary by fuse box and equipment. The reference diagrams identify components such as the A/C compressor clutch relay, condenser fan relay, radiator cooling fan relay, horn relay and blower motor relay.

View relay layout reference

Diagnostic reminder

A fuse or relay label does not prove that the component is faulty. Check power supply, ground, wiring, connectors and the relevant circuit using the correct wiring diagram. Never fit a higher-rated fuse. Disconnect power where the manufacturer's procedure requires it, and use appropriate precautions around high-current circuits.
