Factory-manual fuse locations for the 2008 Honda Pilot. Use the diagrams to find the fuse position, then search the tables for its number, amp rating and protected circuit.

Safety: Confirm the fuse number and rating against the diagram and fuse-box cover on your own vehicle before replacing it. Never install a fuse with a higher amp rating. If a replacement fuse blows again, have the electrical fault diagnosed.

How to use this page

  1. Choose the diagram for the fuse box you are checking.
  2. Find the number printed beside the fuse position in the diagram.
  3. Search that number or the circuit name above, then check the amp rating.
  4. Before touching a fuse, switch the vehicle and affected electrical equipment off and follow the owner's manual procedure.

Source: 2008 Honda Pilot Owner's Manual, “Fuse Locations,” printed pages 289–290. This page does not add relay positions that are not shown on those pages.
