How to Diagnose a Sensor Fault Properly

A fault code is the starting point of diagnosis, not the final answer.

Recommended Diagnostic Workflow

  1. Confirm the complaint: Find out when the fault happens and whether it is constant or intermittent.
  2. Scan the relevant modules: Record trouble codes and freeze-frame data before clearing anything.
  3. Check the basics: Inspect connectors, damaged wires, corrosion, fuses, grounds and power supply.
  4. Read live data: Check whether the sensor reading makes sense under the current conditions.
  5. Test the circuit: Use the correct wiring diagram, meter settings and procedure for that sensor.
  6. Check related components: Leaks, mechanical timing, blocked passages and damaged tone rings can mimic sensor faults.
  7. Repair and verify: Recheck live data and confirm that the original complaint has been resolved.

Rough idleMAF, MAP, ECT, TPSVacuum leaks, ignition faults, compression and fuel trims

ABS warning lightWheel-speed sensorsWiring, wheel-bearing encoder, tone ring and ABS module
