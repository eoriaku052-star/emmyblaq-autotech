Understanding the Work

Automotive Electrical Diagnosis

Electrical problems can involve wiring, sensors, switches, connectors and vehicle control systems. Diagnosis helps narrow down the source of a problem before repair work is recommended.

Vehicle Air-Conditioning Diagnosis

When a car AC system is not cooling properly, possible areas for inspection include refrigerant leakage, compressor operation, electrical controls and other AC components.

Why Vehicle Details Matter

Two vehicles of the same model may have different configurations depending on their year, engine and production version. Accurate vehicle details help guide the inspection process.

Request a Similar Service

If your vehicle has an electrical or air-conditioning problem, these details can help us understand your enquiry:

  • Vehicle make and model.
  • Vehicle year, if known.
  • The problem you have noticed.
  • When the problem started or when it occurs.
  • Any recent repairs related to the problem.

Every vehicle requires appropriate inspection. A video of another vehicle cannot replace diagnosis of your own vehicle.
