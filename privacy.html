Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EmmyBlaQ AutoTech. We respect the privacy of visitors to our website and aim to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website or contact us.

2. Information You Provide

If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, you may choose to provide information such as your name, contact details and information about your vehicle or service enquiry. Please share only information necessary for your enquiry.

3. Information Collected Automatically

Depending on the website services in use, technical information such as your browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited and general usage data may be collected by hosting, analytics or advertising services.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used by this website or its third-party service providers to support website functionality, understand website usage, remember preferences or deliver and measure advertisements. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect certain features.

This website may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to display advertisements if the site is approved and the services are enabled. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalise or measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and provider policies.

Google and its advertising partners may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings. For more information about Google's data practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

6. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Provide information about our automotive services.
  • Maintain website security and performance.
  • Understand general website usage and improve our content.
  • Support advertising and measure its performance where enabled.

We do not intend to sell personal contact information provided directly to us. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website or communication services, or disclosed where required by law. Our website may link to third-party websites or display embedded content, including YouTube videos. When you interact with those services, their providers may collect information under their own policies. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to handle information responsibly. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained only as needed for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have provided to us. You may contact us using the details below to make a privacy enquiry.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation where required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us so we can review the matter.

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. Any update will be published on this page with a revised date.
