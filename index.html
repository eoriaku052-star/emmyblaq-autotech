Automotive Tips & Guides

Why Is Your Car Battery Always Going Flat?

A car battery that repeatedly goes flat may have a charging system problem, a weak battery or an electrical drain when the vehicle is parked. A proper diagnosis can help identify the cause.

Why Is Your Car AC Not Cooling Properly?

Poor AC cooling can result from restricted airflow, a faulty cooling fan, refrigerant leaks or problems with the compressor and other AC components. A qualified technician should inspect the system before repairs are carried out.

Why Does Your Car Refuse to Start?

Starting problems can involve the battery, starter motor, electrical connections, fuel delivery or engine management system. Observing the symptoms and testing the relevant systems can help narrow down the fault.

What Is a Vehicle Diagnostic Scanner?

A diagnostic scanner communicates with compatible vehicle control modules to retrieve diagnostic trouble codes and, depending on the vehicle and scanner, display live operating information. A fault code helps guide diagnosis but does not automatically identify the failed component.
