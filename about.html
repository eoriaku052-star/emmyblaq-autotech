ABOUT OUR BUSINESS

Getting to Know EmmyBlaQ AutoTech

Automotive electrical diagnosis, car air-conditioning services and vehicle technology support in Benin City, Edo State.

Who We Are

EmmyBlaQ AutoTech is an automotive service business based in Idokpa, Benin City, Edo State. We focus on helping vehicle owners investigate electrical problems, repair wiring faults, diagnose air-conditioning issues and install selected vehicle security and control systems.

A vehicle electrical problem can sometimes affect more than one component. A warning light, starting difficulty, faulty sensor or an air-conditioning problem may require proper inspection before the cause can be identified. Our approach is to investigate the affected system rather than assume that the first suspected component is the problem.

Our approach: Inspect the vehicle, test the affected system, explain the findings and provide a quotation before carrying out any agreed repair.

What We Work On

Our services cover several areas of automotive electrical work and vehicle air-conditioning systems.

Automotive Electrical Systems

Electrical fault diagnosis, wiring repairs, battery and charging system diagnosis, and investigation of electrical component problems.

Car Air Conditioning

Car AC diagnosis and repairs, leak detection, and replacement of faulty compressors and other AC components where required.

Vehicle Diagnostics

Vehicle computer scanning, injector system diagnosis, and sensor diagnosis and replacement.

Vehicle Security and Controls

Tracker installation, speed limiter installation, alarm system installation and central lock installation.

How We Handle a Vehicle Job

01. Inspection

We discuss the reported problem and inspect the affected vehicle system.

02. Testing and Diagnosis

Appropriate checks and tests are carried out to investigate the possible cause of the problem.

03. Explanation and Quotation

We explain the findings and provide a quotation for the proposed work before proceeding.

04. Agreed Repair

Work begins after the customer approves the quotation. Appropriate checks are carried out after the agreed repair.
