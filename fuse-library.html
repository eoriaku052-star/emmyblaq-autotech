Important: Confirm Your Exact Vehicle

Fuse and relay layouts can differ according to production year, generation, engine, trim level and market. A diagram for a similar vehicle may not match yours. The links here lead to external reference pages; they are not diagrams created or independently verified by EmmyBlaQ AutoTech.

  1. Confirm your vehicle's production year and generation.
  2. Check whether the reference matches your engine and market.
  3. Compare the fuse-box cover or vehicle manual where available.
  4. Use only the specified fuse rating. Never install a higher-rated fuse.
