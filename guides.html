Automotive Learning Centre

Simple guides to help you understand your vehicle better.

🔋

1. Signs of a Weak Car Battery

Slow engine cranking, dim lights and repeated starting problems can indicate a weak battery or a fault in the charging system.

Tip: Have the battery and alternator tested before replacing parts.

💡

2. Common Electrical Problems

Blown fuses, damaged wiring, loose connections and faulty switches can cause electrical components to stop working.

Tip: Find the cause of a blown fuse instead of repeatedly replacing it.

❄️

3. Why Your Car AC May Not Cool

Possible causes include refrigerant leaks, a faulty compressor, blocked airflow or electrical problems.

Tip: Ask a qualified AC technician to inspect the system.

🖥️

4. Understanding Dashboard Warning Lights

Warning lights alert you to possible problems with systems such as the engine, brakes, battery or oil pressure.

Tip: Check your vehicle manual. Stop safely when a warning indicates a serious problem.

🔍

5. What Is Vehicle Diagnostics?

Diagnostic equipment reads information from a vehicle's computer to help identify possible faults.

Tip: A fault code helps with diagnosis but does not always identify the exact faulty part.

⚡

6. Understanding the Alternator

The alternator helps supply electrical power while the engine runs and recharges the battery.

Tip: Charging problems should be checked with suitable testing equipment.

🌡️

7. Preventing Engine Overheating

Low coolant, leaks, cooling fan faults and thermostat problems can contribute to engine overheating.

Tip: If the engine overheats, stop somewhere safe and let it cool. Never open a hot radiator cap.

📍

8. What Is a Vehicle Tracker?

A vehicle tracker can help an owner monitor a vehicle's location, depending on the device and its features.

Tip: Choose a suitable device and ensure installation and tracking comply with applicable privacy laws.

Important: These guides provide general information, not a substitute for professional inspection. Vehicle systems differ by make and model.
