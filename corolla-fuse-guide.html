How to use the diagram

  1. Confirm that the manual matches your Corolla's year and version.
  2. Identify the correct fuse box: engine compartment or interior.
  3. Match the fuse label and position with the diagram.
  4. Check the specified amperage on the manual and fuse-box cover.
  5. Switch off the ignition and affected electrical equipment before inspection.
